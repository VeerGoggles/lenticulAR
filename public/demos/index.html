pARelax & lenticulAR

Two ways to turn an ordinary screen into a window, both driven by face tracking that runs in the browser. Start a demo, allow the camera, and move your head.

pARelax

A depth-mapped photo behind the glass: as your head moves, the picture shifts in parallax like a view through a window.

lenticulAR

Three depth-mapped scenes and a lenticular flip: move across the screen and a row of rotating prisms masks one scene out and the next one in.

  • Camera frames never leave your device: tracking runs locally with MindAR and MediaPipe.
  • No camera, or would rather not? lenticulAR also follows your mouse or finger.
  • Running both at once tracks your face twice — fine on a laptop; on a phone, open one full screen.
